UPLB Eliens Tryouts

Thu 22 October 2026
By katipuzer0

1st Semester AY 2026-2027

Like solving problems with code? The UPLB Eliens, UPLB’s competitive programming team, are looking for six new members.

Students from any degree program are welcome, and no contest experience is required. You just need to be comfortable with at least one of these languages: C, C++, Java, or Python.

When: Wednesday, October 28, 2026, 1:00 to 4:00 PM Where: ICS PC Lab 3, Institute of Computer Science Format: 7 problems, 2 hours, solo, on DOMjudge

Slots are limited to 24, so register early: https://forms.gle/JtN7Z9FPvnYnxsS28

Full mechanics: https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NFPK7evurTNQraRBKQe0NdQzifEYQgXp1DgsxCYaeEY/edit

For more information, find pataponz and terrdapear on Discord.

See you!
